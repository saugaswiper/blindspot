**Blockers:** none (no secrets, no ops). Uses the 9-review SYNERGY fixture `spec/validation/fixtures/015-synergy-labels.json` and `015-harness.test.ts`.

## Goal
Replace `estimateRecall` (`lib/screening-ranker.ts:142`) with a stopping estimate that is **valid and useful**: it lets a reviewer stop well before 95% of the pool is screened, and it reports a real interval, not a 2× heuristic. Backend library only.

## Why now
Validation 015: the current estimate first reaches 0.95 only after 95–98% of records are screened, and never does for Sep_2021. The same rankings allowed 19–82% of the pool to be skipped at true 95% recall. The point estimate also overestimated true recall in 3–5% of rounds (Sep_2021, Chou_2004). Cause: it sums uncalibrated per-record probabilities over thousands of unscreened records. The stopping rule is the main reason to rank at all (operator requirement: 3,000+ records), so a rule that saves no work is a broken foundation.

## Scope
**In**
- Calibrate the scores before summing, for example Platt scaling or isotonic fit on the reviewer's own screened verdicts, or replace the sum with a sample-based or knee/SAFE-style rule. The dev may choose and must justify it in the handoff.
- Return `{ estimate, lower, upper, method, advisory: true }`, where `lower` and `upper` come from a stated interval method such as a bootstrap or a binomial bound on a random-sample audit. `lower` must be a real bound, not `foundIncludes / (found + 2 × missed)`.
- Keep the existing `rankRecords` and `wss` signatures unchanged. Adding an optional parameter is fine.
- Add a latency guard test: `rankRecords` at n = 3,000 completes in the stated budget (validation 015 saw minutes per run at n ≈ 1,000 from refitting 200 epochs per call). Warm-start or cap the epochs if needed. If that goes beyond this brief, record it in the handoff as a follow-up and do not widen scope.

**Out**
- Global model, training scripts, UI, any route wiring, ASReview baseline comparison, RoB, other screening changes.

## Acceptance criteria (beat, don't match)
Measured with the 015 protocol (1 random include + 1 random exclude prior, batch B = max(10, n/60), seeds 1–3, the same 9 SYNERGY reviews). Mark each `claimed` until the tester reruns.
1. **Useful.** Per review, the **lower bound** first reaches ≥ 0.95 after at most **70% of records screened**, in at least 7 of 9 reviews. Baseline: 97–99.8%, and never for Sep_2021. For reference, a 95%-recall ordering oracle would stop at 18–81% screened.
2. **Valid.** The lower bound is ≤ true recall in at least **95% of (run, round) pairs** after the first 10% of the pool is screened, with the Wilson 95% lower limit on per-run coverage ≥ 85%. The point estimate overshoots true recall by more than 0.05 in ≤ 2% of rounds (baseline: 3–5% overshoot of any size).
3. **Honest interval.** `upper − lower` is reported and shrinks as screening proceeds. At 50% screened the median width is ≤ 0.25.
4. **Advisory only.** Output carries `advisory: true`. Nothing excludes a record automatically.
5. **Latency.** `rankRecords` at n = 3,000 finishes inside the budget the dev states in the test. The budget is at most 10 s per call on the dev machine.
