# Brief 018 — Screening workbench: one verdict indicator per row, demote the right-side label

**Track:** design
**Source:** spec/design/003 §S5 (the 3× verdict restatement). S1–S4, S6–S9, S11, S12 shipped in handoffs 092/093/131; S5 and S10 were deferred in 093 §3 and excluded from brief 014. S5 is the oldest open item.
**Priority:** after any open FAIL-fix brief and Priority NEXT (brief 017 first)
**Date:** 2026-11-04
**Blockers:** none (UI only, no secrets, no ops). A live pixel pass still needs a seeded owned result with 3,000+ decisions (design 003 "Verification basis"). Mark visual ACs `[live-pending]` if that seed does not exist.

## Goal
Each results row restates the effective verdict three times: the 2 px left border (`ScreeningPanel.tsx` ~L678), the round icon badge (~L685), and a filled right-side text pill (~L826). The right-side column is the busiest part of the row and it is entirely non-interactive. Remove the redundancy so the reviewer's own verdict buttons (the action they repeat thousands of times) are the clearest control, without removing any information a reviewer or auditor needs.

## Scope
**In** (one component, one section: the right-hand cluster of a results row)
1. **Demote the verdict text pill.** The right-side `<span>` that renders `tone.label` with `toneBadgeStyle(verdict)` becomes plain text: no filled background, no border, `var(--muted)` colour, same `text-[10px]` size, still visible at rest. The word must stay. The left badge is icon-only, so the text is the non-colour, non-glyph cue for the verdict and also what a sighted screen-reader-off reviewer reads.
2. **Keep the left border and the left icon badge unchanged.** They are the single at-a-glance verdict indicator. No change to `tone`, `toneBadgeStyle` or `DECISION_TONES`.
3. **Order the cluster for scanning.** Top to bottom in the right column: confidence bar, the demoted verdict word, then the "✓ Human" badge when set. Only the pill styling changes. Do not move the `Your verdict` button group or change its size, spacing, `aria-pressed`, tooltips or handlers (28 px targets from S4 stay).
4. **Make the human override legible, not quieter.** When `d.human_decision` is set, the verdict word reads as the effective (human) verdict and the existing "AI said: {d.decision}" line next to the buttons and the "✓ Human" badge stay exactly as they are. Do not collapse the AI verdict into the human one.

**Out**
- S10 (idle CTA), the left border or badge, the row's overall layout or breakpoints, the verdict button group, S9 confidence encoding, list virtualisation, key bindings, any API or ranker change.
- Hiding, shortening or reordering the AI verdict, confidence bar, "✓ Human" badge, retraction badge, `AI said:` text, or any `check` / `manual` / `claimed` marker. This brief only restyles one pill.

## Acceptance criteria
Mark each `claimed` until the tester verifies.
1. **One filled verdict indicator.** In the rendered row, exactly one filled verdict-coloured element remains (the left icon badge) plus the 2 px left border. The right-side verdict word has no background fill and no border. Verify by inspecting the computed style of the right-side word in the handoff.
2. **Contrast.** The demoted verdict word is `var(--muted)` on `--surface`. Report the measured ratio in light and dark; it must be ≥ 4.5:1 (WCAG 1.4.3) in both, or use `--foreground` if `--muted` falls short. State the numbers.
3. **No information lost.** The verdict word is still rendered visibly on every row for include, exclude and uncertain. Rows with `human_decision`, retraction, low confidence, and `check` / `manual` / `claimed` markers show every element they showed before, in the same DOM order (compare the row DOM before and after; the only diff is the one span's class/style).
4. **Verdict controls untouched.** The `Your verdict` group still has three buttons, each ≥ 28 × 28 px (WCAG 2.5.8), `aria-pressed` correct, same tab order. `y/n/u` and `j/k` behaviour from handoff 131 is unchanged.
5. **Keyboard path.** Tab reaches one row stop, then the row's interactive children in the existing order; no new focusable element, no focus-trap (WCAG 2.1.2); active outline is still `var(--ring)`.
6. **Responsive.** At 400 px and 1280 px: no horizontal page scroll introduced; the right column does not wrap onto the title or clip the verdict word. `[live-pending]` if no seed.
7. **Themes.** Light and dark both checked; no hardcoded colour added (`grep` the diff for `#` and `rgb`: zero hits).
8. **Gate.** `tsc --noEmit` and `eslint components/ScreeningPanel.tsx` both exit 0; `npm test` shows no new failures versus the baseline in handoff 131 (12 pre-existing).

## Risk
If the right-hand text turns out to be the only place a reviewer can read the verdict at 400 px (the left badge is `w-5 h-5`, icon only), keep the word and stop. Do not delete it to save width. Record the finding for a follow-up row-layout brief.
